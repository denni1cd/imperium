# Final Output Contract

Every completed Imperial Council session must return one definitive Council result.

## Required sections

### Council Decision
A concise statement of what the Council has decided should be done.

### Why This Strategy Won
Explain which arguments, evidence, tradeoffs, concessions, or syntheses caused this strategy to survive the deliberation.

### Action Plan
Provide a coherent sequence of concrete next steps. The plan should be directly executable by the Sovereign or usable as the basis for subsequent implementation work.

### Critical Assumptions and Tradeoffs
State the important assumptions the plan relies on and the costs or risks the Council knowingly accepts.

### Decision Triggers
Identify evidence, events, thresholds, or changed conditions that should cause the Council's strategy to be reconsidered.

### Minority Objections
Preserve material objections that survived the decision process. These are warnings and reservations, not competing official plans.

## Quality rules

The final result must:

- be internally coherent;
- faithfully implement the strategy that actually won;
- resolve incompatible alternatives rather than list them for the Sovereign to decide;
- distinguish decided actions from unresolved facts or assumptions;
- retain meaningful dissent without weakening the definitiveness of the Council decision;
- avoid claiming consensus when a vote or Seneschal tie-break determined the outcome.

## Fidelity review

Before delivery, participating councillors may flag a concrete mismatch between the drafted plan and the decided strategy. This review is not a new strategic vote and does not reopen settled disagreement unless the mismatch exposes genuinely new information.
