# Evaluations

Evaluation exists to answer the Imperial Council's central question: **does consequential multi-agent deliberation produce a materially better actionable strategy than a single capable adviser?**

## Required comparison modes

Every mature evaluation case should be runnable in three conditions:

1. **Single Adviser** — one capable model produces its best strategy.
2. **Independent Panel** — the same councillors analyze independently but do not debate; a neutral synthesis combines their work.
3. **Imperial Council** — independent counsel, full disclosure, consequential debate, decision closure, and one coherent final plan.

## Primary criterion

Strategic quality and actionability of the final plan.

Diagnostic measures may include:

- diversity of initial interpretations;
- observable influence of value matrices;
- specificity of cross-member engagement;
- defenses, concessions, revisions, and syntheses;
- whether debate changes the decision state;
- preservation of meaningful dissent;
- successful termination;
- final-plan coherence;
- usefulness of decision triggers.

These diagnostics must never replace the primary question by rewarding theatrical disagreement, verbosity, or raw agent activity.
